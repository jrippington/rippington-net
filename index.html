Approach

Delegate the work, not the judgement

Not every problem needs an LLM, and not every workflow needs an agent.

I start with the problem, the software and the people. AI gets used where it earns its place, and the engineering judgement stays with people who have it. Sometimes the answer is an agent. Sometimes it’s conventional software. Often it’s both.
