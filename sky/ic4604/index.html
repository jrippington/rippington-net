The Rho Ophiuchi Clouds

The nearest cluster of newborn stars

Years: how old its light is
450
Image
1
First taken

Clouds of dust and gas about 450 light-years away, one of the two nearest places where stars are being born, lit blue, yellow, and red by the stars round them, Antares among them.

The Rho Ophiuchi clouds are named for the star at their northern end, whose light the dust round it reflects as the blue nebula IC 4604. Inside the darkest of them, hidden from ordinary light, is the richest cluster of newborn stars within about a thousand light-years, and beside them, the red supergiant Antares lights the dust yellow.
