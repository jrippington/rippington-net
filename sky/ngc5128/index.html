The nearest radio galaxy, about 12 million light-years away in Centaurus: a giant galaxy made by a merger, with a warped lane of dust across it and a jet from its black hole.

Centaurus A, NGC 5128, is a giant galaxy about 12 million light-years away in Centaurus, and the nearest radio galaxy to us. A merger about 2 billion years ago seems to have made it; a warped disc of gas and dust lies across its face, and the black hole at its centre, about 55 million times the Sun’s mass, fires a jet that lights clouds of gas tens of thousands of light-years out. James Dunlop found it from New South Wales in 1826.
