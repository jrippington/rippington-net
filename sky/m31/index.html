The nearest big galaxy to our own, a spiral larger than the Milky Way, about 2.5 million light-years away in Andromeda, with its companions M32 and M110. It's visible to the naked eye from a dark site.

The Andromeda Galaxy, M31, is the nearest big galaxy to ours: a spiral larger than the Milky Way, with something like a trillion stars, about 2.5 million light-years away. It’s coming towards us, and in 1923 Edwin Hubble found the variable star in it that showed the spiral nebulae are galaxies of their own.
