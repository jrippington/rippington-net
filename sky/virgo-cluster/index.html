The nearest large cluster of galaxies, about 54 million light-years away in Virgo, centred on the giant elliptical M87 and its black hole, with Markarian's Chain curving across its core.

The Virgo Cluster is the nearest large cluster of galaxies, about 54 million light-years away: well over a thousand galaxies, centred on the giant elliptical M87, whose black hole was the first ever photographed. Across its core curves Markarian’s Chain.
