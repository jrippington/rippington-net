IC 1396 is a great cloud of glowing hydrogen nearly 3 degrees across, about 3,000 light-years away in Cepheus, lit by the hot stars of the young cluster Trumpler 37. On the shell round it sit more than 20 dark globules with bright rims, the best known of them the Elephant’s Trunk.
