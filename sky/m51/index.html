A grand-design spiral seen face on, about 25 to 28 million light-years away in Canes Venatici, with its companion NGC 5195 at the end of one arm: the first spiral ever recognised.

The Whirlpool Galaxy, M51, is a grand-design spiral seen face on, about 25 to 28 million light-years away in Canes Venatici. Its companion, NGC 5195, sits at the end of one of its arms; the two have met within the last few hundred million years, and the encounter has probably raised the Whirlpool’s two strong arms and pulled faint streams of stars out of both. Lord Rosse saw its spiral in 1845, the first ever recognised.
