Bode's Galaxy, a grand spiral about 12 million light-years away in Ursa Major, the largest of the M81 group, its arms perhaps raised by its companions' passage.

Bode’s Galaxy, M81, is a grand spiral about 12 million light-years away in Ursa Major, about 94,000 light-years across, and the largest galaxy of the M81 group. A few hundred million years ago its companions, M82 and NGC 3077, swung past it; the encounters tore bridges of hydrogen out of all three, and may have raised its spiral arms. Johann Bode found it, with M82, on 31 December 1774.
