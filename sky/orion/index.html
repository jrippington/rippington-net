Orion, the Hunter, with its bright stars about 250 to 1,250 light-years away, and behind its belt and sword the nearest place where massive stars are forming, in the middle of the spiral arm of the Galaxy named after it.

Orion, the Hunter, is one of the best-known constellations in the sky, and everyone on Earth can see it, since its belt lies almost on the celestial equator. Its bright stars are about 250 to 1,250 light-years away, and behind the belt and sword are the clouds of the Orion Nebula, the nearest place where massive stars are forming, in the middle of the spiral arm of the Galaxy named after it.
