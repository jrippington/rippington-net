A spiral about 24.7 million light-years away in Canes Venatici, whose distance, measured by geometry from masers orbiting its black hole, anchors the cosmic distance scale; jets from its nucleus shock its disc into glowing anomalous arms.

M106, NGC 4258, is a spiral galaxy about 24.7 million light-years away in Canes Venatici. Water masers orbiting the black hole at its centre give its distance by pure geometry, which makes it one of the anchors of the cosmic distance scale, and jets from its nucleus shock the gas of its disc into two glowing, anomalous arms.
