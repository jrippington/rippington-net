Stephan's Quintet

A collision of galaxies, and one in front

Four galaxies colliding about 290 million light-years away, and a fifth, NGC 7320, much nearer, in front of them: the first compact group of galaxies ever found, in Pegasus.

Stephan’s Quintet is the first compact group of galaxies ever found, published by Édouard Stephan from Marseille in 1877. Four of its galaxies are tangled together about 290 million light-years away, where one falling in at high speed is driving a shock wave through the group’s gas. The fifth, NGC 7320, is between about 40 and 50 million light-years away, and only happens to lie in the same direction.
