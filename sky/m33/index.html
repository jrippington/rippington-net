The third great galaxy of our Local Group, a loose spiral about 2.7 million light-years away in Triangulum, with no central bulge and no giant black hole, and one of the most distant things the eye can see.

The Triangulum Galaxy, M33, is the third-largest galaxy of our Local Group, after Andromeda and the Milky Way: a loose spiral about 2.7 million light-years away, with no central bulge and no giant black hole. Under a really dark sky it’s one of the most distant things the eye can see.
