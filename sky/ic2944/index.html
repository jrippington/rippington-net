The Running Chicken Nebula

A young cluster, and the small dark clouds in its glare

Years: how old its light is
7,600
Images
2
First taken
2023

A cloud of glowing hydrogen about 7,600 light-years away in Centaurus, lit by a young cluster of hot, massive stars. Its name is informal, and nobody agrees which part of it is the chicken.

Royal Frost found it in May 1904, on a photograph taken at Harvard’s southern station at Arequipa, in Peru. The hot young stars at its heart light the whole cloud, and in front of the glow are Thackeray’s globules, small dark clouds that the starlight is slowly evaporating.
