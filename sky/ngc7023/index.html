A reflection nebula about 1,100 light-years away in Cepheus: dust in a dark cloud, lit blue by a young, hot double star, and the first place neutral buckminsterfullerene was firmly found in space.

The Iris Nebula, NGC 7023, is a reflection nebula about 1,100 light-years away in Cepheus: dust in the dark cloud LDN 1174, lit blue by the young, hot double star HD 200775. In 2010 it became the first place in interstellar space where neutral C60, buckminsterfullerene, was firmly found.
