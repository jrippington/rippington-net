The Statue of Liberty Nebula

A hidden cluster, and the bubble its stars have blown

Years: how old its light is
9,000
Images
2
First taken
2023

A giant cloud of glowing gas about 9,000 light-years away in Carina, round a cluster of young, massive stars buried so deep in dust that none of them can be seen in visible light.

John Herschel found it from the Cape of Good Hope in March 1834, and gave its pieces six numbers. Its young stars have blown a bubble above the cloud they formed in, and in some pictures the bubble and the dark pillars inside it look like the Statue of Liberty.
