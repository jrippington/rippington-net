A great cloud of glowing hydrogen in Cassiopeia, about 6,800 light-years away, hollowed out by the young cluster Melotte 15 at its centre, whose hottest stars light it.

The Heart Nebula, IC 1805, is a cloud of hydrogen about 300 light-years across, lit by the young cluster Melotte 15 at its centre, whose hottest stars have blown a cavity about 130 light-years wide in the gas round them. It’s about 6,800 light-years away, in the Perseus Arm, the next spiral arm out from ours, beside the Soul Nebula and the Fish Head.
