Thor's Helmet, a bubble blown by the wind of a dying star, about 13,400 light-years away in Canis Major, with a wing of glowing gas on each side.

Thor’s Helmet, NGC 2359, is a bubble about 18 light-years across, about 13,400 light-years away in Canis Major, blown by the fast wind of WR 7, a Wolf-Rayet star stripped to its hot interior. Most of it is interstellar gas the wind has swept up; the wings either side are the gas round it, lit by the same star, and dense molecular clouds to its east and south have made it lopsided. William Herschel found it in 1785.
