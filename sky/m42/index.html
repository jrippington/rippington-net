The misty patch in Orion's sword, about 1,270 light-years away: the nearest place where massive stars are forming.

The Orion Nebula is the misty patch in Orion’s sword, visible to the naked eye, about 1,270 light-years away: the nearest place where massive stars are forming. It’s lit by the Trapezium, the young cluster at its heart, and in 1880 it was the first nebula ever photographed.
