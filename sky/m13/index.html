The Great Globular Cluster in Hercules, M13, is an ancient ball of stars about 484,000 times the Sun’s mass and about 12 billion years old, about 24,200 light-years away. It’s one of the brightest globular clusters in the northern sky, and in 1974 the Arecibo radio telescope aimed a message at it.
