An ancient ball of stars about 410,000 times the Sun's mass, about 33,200 light-years away in the Milky Way's halo, in Canes Venatici, with more RR Lyrae variable stars than any other globular cluster.

M3, NGC 5272, is a globular cluster: an ancient ball of stars about 410,000 times the Sun’s mass, about 33,200 light-years away, high above the Milky Way’s disc. It holds more RR Lyrae variable stars than any other globular cluster, and it was the first object Charles Messier discovered himself, in 1764.
