A reflection nebula about 1,300 light-years away in Orion: dust in the northern part of the Orion B cloud, lit blue by a pair of young, hot stars, in a little group of nebulae round a young cluster.

M78, NGC 2068, is a reflection nebula about 1,300 light-years away in Orion: dust in the northern part of the Orion B cloud, the cloud of the Horsehead, lit blue by a pair of young, hot B stars. It’s the brightest of a little group of reflection nebulae there, with NGC 2064, NGC 2067, and NGC 2071, round a young cluster about 2 million years old, in a cloud still busy with the jets of stars being born.
