The telescopes, Optica

iTelescope T59

A 20-inch reflector at Siding Spring

March 2022, 2 min read

On this page

  1. The telescope and the camera
  2. In numbers

T59 is a PlaneWave CDK20, a 0.51 m reflector at its full focal length of 3.4 m, with a large CCD camera, on PlaneWave's Ascension 200HR mount at Siding Spring Observatory in Australia. Its frame is 37′ square, a little larger than the full Moon, at 0.54″ per pixel: a telescope for small, detailed targets.

I started using iTelescope in 2022-2023, primarily to explore the southern skies while I was living in Central London. T59 is one of iTelescope’s telescopes at Siding Spring Observatory, in New South Wales, 31° south of the equator.

The telescope and the camera

The CDK20 is a corrected Dall-Kirkham: a 20-inch concave primary mirror, a convex secondary, and two lenses before the focus that keep the field flat and the stars round to its edges. T59 uses it without a reducer, at f/6.8 and 3,411 mm. The black cloth round its truss is a shroud, which keeps stray light off the mirrors.

The camera is an FLI ProLine 16803, with a KAF-16803 CCD 4096 pixels square; at this focal length each 9 µm pixel covers 0.54 arcseconds. The mount tracks well enough that iTelescope run T59 without guiding, with exposures of up to 15 minutes.
