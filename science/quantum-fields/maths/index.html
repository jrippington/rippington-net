Lumps. Each mode is a held-in wave, a harmonic oscillator of frequency ω\omegaω, and the quantum rules allow it only the energies

No labels. Two lumps, one in mode k1k_1k1​ and one in mode k2k_2k2​, are the single state “one lump in k1k_1k1​, one in k2k_2k2​”; there is no second state with them the other way round, because a lump has no identity apart from its mode. That is why swapping two identical particles changes nothing. For electrons the field’s lumps obey a further rule, that a mode can hold at most one: the eleventh journey’s exclusion principle, built into the electron field.

The threshold for making a pair

Rest energies. An electron’s rest energy is mec2=0.511m_e c^2 = 0.511me​c2=0.511 MeV (an electronvolt is 1.602×10−191.602 \times 10^{-19}1.602×10−19 joules), and a positron’s is the same. To make the pair, a gamma ray must supply at least

2mec2=1.022 MeV.2 m_e c^2 = 1.022\ \text{MeV}.2me​c2=1.022 MeV.

Why it needs a nucleus. A photon alone has energy and momentum in the ratio E=pcE = pcE=pc. The pair it would become, carrying the same momentum ppp, has energy at least

(2mec2)2+(pc)2>pc,\sqrt{(2 m_e c^2)^2 + (pc)^2} > pc ,(2me​c2)2+(pc)2​>pc,

so energy and momentum can’t both be kept. Near a nucleus of mass MMM, the nucleus takes up the spare momentum for almost no energy, and the threshold becomes 2mec2(1+me/M)2 m_e c^2 (1 + m_e/M)2me​c2(1+me​/M), a hair above 1.022 MeV.

with KKK its kinetic energy. In convenient units, r=pc/(299.79 B)r = pc / (299.79\, B)r=pc/(299.79B) metres with pcpcpc in MeV and BBB in tesla: an electron with 1 MeV of motion (pc=1.42pc = 1.42pc=1.42 MeV) in a field of 0.1 tesla curls round a circle about 4.7 centimetres in radius. Positive and negative charges curl opposite ways; as they slow in the chamber’s gas, ppp falls, and they spiral inwards.

Back again. An electron and a positron meeting at rest have no momentum between them, so they can’t become a single photon; they become two, flying apart back to back, each with mec2=0.511m_e c^2 = 0.511me​c2=0.511 MeV. A PET scanner’s ring catches exactly such pairs.

The Casimir push

Between two perfectly conducting plates a distance ddd apart, the electromagnetic field’s modes must have a whole number of half-waves across the gap, kz=nπ/dk_z = n\pi/dkz​=nπ/d, while outside they may have any. Each mode keeps its zero-point energy 12ℏω\tfrac12\hbar\omega21​ℏω. The total is infinite on both sides, but the difference between them is finite, and Casimir (1948) found the energy per area of the plates to be

EA=−π2ℏc720 d3,\frac{E}{A} = -\frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{720\, d^3},AE​=−720d3π2ℏc​,

so the plates are pressed together with a pressure

P=π2ℏc240 d4.P = \frac{\pi^2 \hbar c}{240\, d^4}.P=240d4π2ℏc​.

At d=10d = 10d=10 nm, a hundred-thousandth of a millimetre, P≈1.3×105P \approx 1.3 \times 10^5P≈1.3×105 Pa, about the pressure of the air. At a thousandth of a millimetre it is 10810^{8}108 times smaller, 1.3×10−31.3 \times 10^{-3}1.3×10−3 Pa. Lamoreaux (1997) used a flat plate and a curved lens, for which the force is π3ℏcR/360d3\pi^3 \hbar c R / 360 d^3π3ℏcR/360d3, RRR the lens’s radius of curvature, at gaps from about 0.6 to 6 micrometres, and agreed with it to about 5 per cent. Robert Jaffe (2005) showed that the same result follows from the forces between the plates’ moving charges, without mentioning the vacuum’s energy, which is why the article says that physicists still argue over the best description.

The proton’s mass budget

The proton. Its mass is 938.27 MeV. Its quarks, two up and one down, have masses of about 2.16 and 4.70 MeV (the Particle Data Group’s values), so

2×2.16+4.70938.27=9.02938.27≈0.96 per cent.\frac{2 \times 2.16 + 4.70}{938.27} = \frac{9.02}{938.27} \approx 0.96\ \text{per cent}.938.272×2.16+4.70​=938.279.02​≈0.96 per cent.

A neutron’s, one up and two down, are 11.56/939.57≈1.211.56 / 939.57 \approx 1.211.56/939.57≈1.2 per cent. Nearly all the rest is the energy of the quarks’ motion and of the gluon field between them: by E=mc2E = mc^2E=mc2, energy in a box is mass. (Counted more carefully, including the quark and antiquark pairs that come and go inside the proton, the share that comes from the quarks’ own masses, which the Higgs field gives them, is a little larger, but still a small part of the whole.)
