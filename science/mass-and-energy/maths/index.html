The energy bill

By Newton’s rule, an object of mass mmm moving at speed vvv has kinetic energy 12mv2\tfrac{1}{2}mv^221​mv2. If that held at every speed, the bill for reaching the speed of light would be

KNewton=12mc2,K_{\text{Newton}} = \tfrac{1}{2}mc^2,KNewton​=21​mc2,

which for The Van, at two and a half tonnes, is 12×2,500×(3.00×108)2=1.1×1020\tfrac{1}{2} \times 2{,}500 \times (3.00 \times 10^8)^2 = 1.1 \times 10^{20}21​×2,500×(3.00×108)2=1.1×1020 joules: about what the whole world used in two months in 2023, at 620 exajoules (6.2×10206.2 \times 10^{20}6.2×1020 joules) for the year.

K=(γ−1) mc2,γ=11−v2/c2.K = (\gamma - 1)\,mc^2, \qquad \gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}.K=(γ−1)mc2,γ=1−v2/c2​1​.

w=v+u1+uv/c2.w = \frac{v + u}{1 + uv/c^2}.w=1+uv/c2v+u​.

The sum of the two pulses

Here are the steps the article’s account passed over. A box at rest emits two pulses of light, each with energy E/2E/2E/2 in its own frame, one forwards and one backwards along the line of a probe that moves past it at speed vvv.

Measured from the probe, the box moves forwards at vvv, and the two pulses are Doppler shifted by the factors

Dforwards=1+v/c1−v/c,Dbackwards=1−v/c1+v/c.D_{\text{forwards}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 + v/c}{1 - v/c}}, \qquad D_{\text{backwards}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - v/c}{1 + v/c}}.Dforwards​=1−v/c1+v/c​​,Dbackwards​=1+v/c1−v/c​​.

At v=0.6cv = 0.6cv=0.6c they are 1.6/0.4=2\sqrt{1.6/0.4} = 21.6/0.4​=2 and 12\tfrac{1}{2}21​, so, measured from the probe,

2×E2⏟forwards  +  12×E2⏟backwards  =  1.25 E.\underbrace{2 \times \tfrac{E}{2}}_{\text{forwards}} \; + \; \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{2} \times \tfrac{E}{2}}_{\text{backwards}} \; = \; 1.25\,E.forwards2×2E​​​+backwards21​×2E​​​=1.25E.

At v=0.8cv = 0.8cv=0.8c they are 1.8/0.2=3\sqrt{1.8/0.2} = 31.8/0.2​=3 and 13\tfrac{1}{3}31​, and the light carries E2(3+13)=53E\tfrac{E}{2}\left(3 + \tfrac{1}{3}\right) = \tfrac{5}{3}E2E​(3+31​)=35​E. In general, add the shifted energies together, over a common denominator:

E2(1+v/c1−v/c+1−v/c1+v/c)=E2⋅(1+v/c)+(1−v/c)1−v2/c2=γE.\frac{E}{2}\left(\sqrt{\frac{1 + v/c}{1 - v/c}} + \sqrt{\frac{1 - v/c}{1 + v/c}}\right) = \frac{E}{2} \cdot \frac{(1 + v/c) + (1 - v/c)}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}} = \gamma E.2E​(1−v/c1+v/c​​+1+v/c1−v/c​​)=2E​⋅1−v2/c2​(1+v/c)+(1−v/c)​=γE.

So, by the article’s accounts, the box’s kinetic energy relative to the probe falls by

ΔK=(γ−1) E.\Delta K = (\gamma - 1)\,E.ΔK=(γ−1)E.

The box’s kinetic energy is (γ−1) mc2(\gamma - 1)\,mc^2(γ−1)mc2, and its speed, and so its γ\gammaγ, doesn’t change. Only its mass can, and if the mass falls by Δm\Delta mΔm, the kinetic energy falls by (γ−1) Δm c2(\gamma - 1)\,\Delta m\,c^2(γ−1)Δmc2. So

(γ−1) Δm c2=(γ−1) E,Δm=Ec2.(\gamma - 1)\,\Delta m\,c^2 = (\gamma - 1)\,E, \qquad \Delta m = \frac{E}{c^2}.(γ−1)Δmc2=(γ−1)E,Δm=c2E​.

The (γ−1)(\gamma - 1)(γ−1) is on both sides, so the answer is the same at every speed.

The pair of pulses, taken together, has the mass the box lost. The mass of a system is fixed by its total energy and total momentum,

Mc2=Etotal2−(ptotal c)2,M c^2 = \sqrt{E_{\text{total}}^2 - (p_{\text{total}}\,c)^2},Mc2=Etotal2​−(ptotal​c)2​,

which comes out the same in every frame. In the box’s frame the pulses have energy EEE and no momentum, so Mc2=EMc^2 = EMc2=E. From the probe they have energy γE\gamma EγE and momentum γβE/c\gamma\beta E/cγβE/c forwards, and γ2E2−γ2β2E2=E\sqrt{\gamma^2E^2 - \gamma^2\beta^2E^2} = Eγ2E2−γ2β2E2​=E again. Each pulse on its own has energy equal to its momentum times ccc, and so no mass at all.

Einstein, in 1905, used the low-speed version. When vvv is small compared with ccc, γ−1≈12v2/c2\gamma - 1 \approx \tfrac{1}{2}v^2/c^2γ−1≈21​v2/c2, so the box’s kinetic energy fell by about 12(E/c2) v2\tfrac{1}{2}(E/c^2)\,v^221​(E/c2)v2. In Newton’s 12mv2\tfrac{1}{2}mv^221​mv2, at a fixed speed, that is just what a fall in mass of E/c2E/c^2E/c2 would do:

E=Δm c2.E = \Delta m\, c^2.E=Δmc2.

E = mc² in numbers

Turned round, the box’s accounts say that every mass carries energy in proportion to it. An object of mass mmm, at rest, has the rest energy

E0=mc2.E_0 = mc^2.E0​=mc2.

Einstein’s own sentence, in the English translation of his paper, is: “If a body gives off the energy LLL in the form of radiation, its mass diminishes by L/c2L/c^2L/c2.” (LLL was his letter for the energy.)
