The atom’s size from the squeeze

The estimate. Hold an electron within a distance rrr of a proton. By the uncertainty principle (the seventh journey) its momentum is at least about ℏ/r\hbar/rℏ/r, so its energy of motion is at least about ℏ2/2mr2\hbar^2/2m r^2ℏ2/2mr2, while the proton’s pull gives it an energy −ke2/r-ke^2/r−ke2/r (with k=1/4πε0k = 1/4\pi\varepsilon_0k=1/4πε0​). Into the nucleus. Why a steady pattern doesn’t shine. A state of definite energy changes in time only by a turning phase, ψ(r,t)=ψ(r) e−iEt/ℏ\psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \psi(\mathbf{r})\, e^{-iEt/\hbar}ψ(r,t)=ψ(r)e−iEt/ℏ, so its density ∣ψ(r,t)∣2=∣ψ(r)∣2|\psi(\mathbf{r}, t)|^2 = |\psi(\mathbf{r})|^2∣ψ(r,t)∣2=∣ψ(r)∣2, and with it the charge spread round the nucleus, doesn’t change at all. Nothing oscillates, so nothing radiates.
