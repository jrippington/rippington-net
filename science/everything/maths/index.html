A white dwarf’s limit

A white dwarf of mass MMM and radius RRR holds N=M/(μemp)N = M / (\mu_e m_p)N=M/(μe​mp​) electrons, where μe≈2\mu_e \approx 2μe​≈2 is the number of protons and neutrons per electron (for carbon and oxygen). Packed into a volume of about R3R^3R3, each electron is squeezed into a space about R/N1/3R / N^{1/3}R/N1/3 across, so by the uncertainty principle its momentum is at least about

p≈ℏN1/3R.p \approx \frac{\hbar N^{1/3}}{R}.p≈RℏN1/3​.

In a heavy white dwarf the electrons are squeezed so hard that they move at nearly the speed of light, and relativity sets each one’s energy at pcpcpc, not p2/2mep^2 / 2m_ep2/2me​. Then the energy pushing out, per electron, is

Epush≈ℏc N1/3R,E_{\text{push}} \approx \frac{\hbar c\, N^{1/3}}{R},Epush​≈RℏcN1/3​,

and gravity’s pull on the matter that goes with each electron is

Epull≈GMμempR.E_{\text{pull}} \approx \frac{G M \mu_e m_p}{R}.Epull​≈RGMμe​mp​​.

Both fall off as 1/R1/R1/R, so shrinking the star doesn’t change which is bigger. If the push wins, the star holds; if the pull wins, shrinking makes nothing better, and it collapses. Where gravity and the quantum meet

Anything of mass mmm is spread, by the quantum rules, over at least about its Compton wavelength,

λC=ℏmc,\lambda_C = \frac{\hbar}{m c},λC​=mcℏ​,

and gravity closes a horizon round it if it’s squeezed inside its Schwarzschild radius, from One Way In,

rs=2Gmc2.r_s = \frac{2 G m}{c^2}.rs​=c22Gm​.

For everything from an electron to a star, the two are wildly different: an electron’s Compton wavelength is 3.9×10−133.9 \times 10^{-13}3.9×10−13 m and its Schwarzschild radius 1.4×10−571.4 \times 10^{-57}1.4×10−57 m, so quantum effects matter and gravity doesn’t; for the Sun it’s the other way round. They become equal, so that both must matter at once, when ℏ/(mc)≈Gm/c2\hbar / (mc) \approx G m / c^2ℏ/(mc)≈Gm/c2, that is at the Planck mass

mP=ℏcG=2.18×10−8 kg,m_P = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar c}{G}} = 2.18 \times 10^{-8}\ \text{kg},mP​=Gℏc​​=2.18×10−8 kg,

about a tenth of the mass of the grain of sugar, squeezed into the Planck length

ℓP=ℏGc3=1.62×10−35 m,\ell_P = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}} = 1.62 \times 10^{-35}\ \text{m},ℓP​=c3ℏG​​=1.62×10−35 m,
