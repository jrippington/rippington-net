The seabirds photographed so far: the herring gulls of Mevagissey harbour in Cornwall, which Cal photographed once we'd finished defending our chips from them.

The photographs of seabirds are below, a species at a time, each with a note on the bird and the day. These are from Mevagissey, on our trips to Cornwall.

Herring gull

Larus argentatus4 photographs

The big silver-grey gull of British harbours and seaside towns, and the scourge of anyone eating chips. Young birds are mottled brown, and take about four years to reach their grey and white adult plumage. Its numbers on the coast have fallen so far that it's on the UK's red list.
