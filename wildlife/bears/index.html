My photographs of bears are below, a species at a time, each with a note on the animal and the day. These are from a visit to Wildwood, in Kent; there are more from there to come.

Eurasian brown bear

Ursus arctos arctos1 photograph

The brown bear of Europe and northern Asia, the most widespread bear in the world. It died out in Britain more than a thousand years ago, and still lives in Scandinavia and Finland, the Carpathians and the Balkans, with a few hundred in Spain, the Pyrenees, the Alps and Italy.
