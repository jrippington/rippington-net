The parrots I've photographed so far are wild ones in London: the rose-ringed parakeets of Bushy Park.

My photographs of parrots are below, a species at a time, each with a note on the bird and the day. These are from an afternoon in Bushy Park.

Rose-ringed parakeet

Psittacula krameri2 photographs

A parrot of a belt of Africa just south of the Sahara, and of South Asia, that has bred wild in Britain since 1969, most of all in and around London, where thousands gather at dusk in noisy roosts. The males have a black and rose-pink ring around the neck.
