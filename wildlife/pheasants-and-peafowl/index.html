The pheasants and peafowl I've photographed so far: one peacock at St Nectan's Glen, in Cornwall, who watched us very suspiciously while we had our coffee.

My photographs of pheasants and peafowl are below, a species at a time, each with a note on the bird and the day. The peacock is from St Nectan’s Glen.

Indian peafowl

Pavo cristatus1 photograph

Native to India, Sri Lanka and their neighbours, peafowl have been kept in parks and gardens far beyond them for more than two thousand years. The peacock's long train of eye-spotted feathers, raised in a fan to display, is shed and grown again every year.
