The Sea of Nectar, Ithil

Cal Above Kynance Cove

June 2022, Photograph, reworked with textures, 8,256 × 5,504 pixels

Draft, for review

A photograph of Cal on the cliffs above Kynance Cove, on the Lizard in Cornwall, which I reworked in Photoshop with textures.

Cal, on the rocks above Kynance Cove, with his camera pointed back at me. I digitally altered the picture afterwards with textures in Photoshop, as I did the sky over Corfe Castle: it’s something I’d like to do more of, making works of art that start from photographs.

The cove itself, and the trip, are on Arda: Kynance Cove.
